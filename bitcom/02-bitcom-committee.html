About us

Bioinformatics and IT as a shared EFI responsibility.

Established in 2018, BITCom advises the EFI Executive Committee and other EFI bodies on bioinformatics, digital infrastructure and information technology. Its work brings together data standards, practical education and sustainable digital services.

Our remit

Advise EFI

Translate technical questions into workable solutions and help EFI make well-informed choices about data, software systems and digital processes.

Support collaboration

Work alongside EFI Standards & Quality Assurance, Accreditation, Scientific and Education Committees, as well as conference organisers.

Keep practice central

Promote secure, reliable and interoperable tools that help European H&I laboratories, rather than adding complexity for its own sake.
